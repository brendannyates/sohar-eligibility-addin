Clinic files and emails

One file per location for the clinic team: patients with an upcoming visit, benefits, flags and a re-verify link. Opening it in Excel shows the Clinic tab. The email summary uses LASFIR and Per Nbr only.

Clinic files contain PHI. Share them only through MHS OneDrive or Teams with the clinic team; do not email them outside MHS.

Payer evidence

Every refresh adds what was seen to the Payer Evidence sheet: NextGen payer, member ID prefix, location, ZIP3 and what Sohar returned. Over time it shows which Sohar payer a one-off NextGen name really is.

IR Portal cross-check (optional)

Upload the IR Portal workbook (its Filtered tab) or a CSV of it. Insurance items are matched to the Sohar results by MRN, then name and DOB, and written to IR Cross-Ref.
