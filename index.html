Sohar eligibility prep

Checking workbook…

  1. 1

    Import the appointment report

    Upload the NextGen Appointments Listing (.csv or .xlsx), or paste the whole report into the Import sheet at A1, title rows included. The header row is found automatically.

  2. 2

    Map payers

    Each NextGen payer name needs a Sohar payer ID. Mapped names are reused every time; only new names show here.

  3. 3

    Build the patient list

    One row per patient. Verification when the payer is mapped and a member ID exists; discovery otherwise. Writes the Patients, Review, Verification and Discovery sheets.

  4. 4

    Export for Sohar

    Column order matches the Sohar templates. Files download from this pane; the same rows are on the Verification and Discovery sheets.

    In Excel on the web, files save from the full-screen window (the pane itself cannot download there).

  5. 5

    Bring back Sohar results

    Upload the verification and discovery result files from Sohar (.csv or .xlsx, several at once is fine). They are matched to the appointment report on the Import sheet and written to the Sohar Results sheet, soonest appointment first.

    Messages list patients by location with LASFIR and Per Nbr: who needs action (including which NextGen insurance to choose after discovery), or whose deductible or OOP max is met or approaching. Send inside Outlook or Teams only. The benefits CSV is for importing into the TMS Tracker.

Data stays in this workbook. The pane makes no network calls and keeps nothing after it closes.
